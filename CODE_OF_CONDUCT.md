# Code of conduct

Klaxond contributors and users should be able to participate without
harassment, intimidation or discrimination.

Be respectful, discuss the work rather than the person, assume good intent
while still addressing harmful behavior, and accept maintainer decisions about
scope and safety. Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
misgendering, publication of private information and sustained disruption are
not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Do not report a conduct concern in a public issue when doing so would expose the
reporter or another person. Use the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/luigibarretta/klaxond/security/advisories/new)
and begin the report title with `Code of conduct`. Include only the information
needed to investigate safely. The maintainer will acknowledge the report within
seven days and will keep its details limited to people needed for resolution.
